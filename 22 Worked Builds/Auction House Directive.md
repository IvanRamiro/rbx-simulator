I noticed that the Market currently serves a very similar purpose to the Merchant, so I want to change its functionality to avoid having two buildings with essentially the same purpose.

**Keep the existing Market building design and structure. I am already satisfied with its appearance, so there is no need to redesign or replace the building. Instead, transform it into an Auction House / Player Trading Center.**

### New Market Functionality: Auction House

The building should become a player-driven marketplace where players can buy and sell eligible items through auctions.

**Core Features:**

1. **Player Listings**
   - Players can list eligible pets and weapons for auction.
   - Sellers can set a starting bid and an auction duration.
   - Display item details, rarity, seller information, and current highest bid.

2. **Bidding System**
   - Other players can place bids using Spirit Stones.
   - The highest valid bidder wins when the auction ends.
   - Players should be able to track active auctions and their current bids.

3. **Rare Collectibles**
   - Make rare, mythical, exclusive, and limited items desirable collectibles.
   - Encourage players to participate in auctions to obtain valuable items they cannot easily acquire elsewhere.

4. **Player-Driven Economy**
   - Introduce an additional way for players to exchange valuable items.
   - Give players more control over how they obtain and distribute rare pets and weapons.
   - Create opportunities for social interaction and a player-driven economy.

### Critical Requirements: Security & Economy Protection

This feature must be designed carefully to prevent:
- Auction manipulation and bid exploits.
- Duplicate item listings.
- Item duplication or unauthorized transfers.
- Fake transactions and fraudulent trades.
- Exploitation of Spirit Stones.
- Last-second bidding exploits and other unfair auction mechanics.

All important transactions must be server-authoritative, properly validated, and securely processed.

### Final Instructions

- Preserve the existing Market building.
- Replace its Merchant-like functionality with an Auction House system.
- Make the interface polished, intuitive, and consistent with the game's existing UI.
- Ensure the system is fully responsive across PC, tablet, and mobile.
- Consider the game's existing economy before introducing new transaction mechanics.
- Avoid creating unnecessary duplication between the Merchant and the new Auction House.

I want this to become a major social and economic feature of the game, giving players a reason to interact, collect rare items, and participate in a player-driven trading economy.
