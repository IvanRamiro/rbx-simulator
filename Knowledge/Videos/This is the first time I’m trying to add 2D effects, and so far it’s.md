## Key takeaways

What the frames show, read as lessons for us. These are my interpretation, not the creator's advice.

- **A smear for a frame or two sells speed:** the limb stretches or breaks into strokes for an instant. It could suit
  the fastest frames of our Blender weapon clips and the guardians' pounces, which currently rely on motion alone.
- **The swirl hides a change of pose:** a burst of strokes covers the cut. Possibly useful for the sword finisher.
- **Its warning:** the creator flags the effect looking worse after export, with a jagged edge. Test any cut-out
  texture in the game itself, not only in the authoring tool.
