## Key takeaways

What the frames show, read as lessons for us. These are my interpretation, not the creator's advice.

- **One choreography, many tiers:** the same motion recoloured and scaled reads as a clear ladder of power. It could
  fit our forged weapon elements or the breakthrough moments, though the owner has asked that effects not be just
  recoloured defaults, so each tier would still need its own idea.
- **The black silhouette against a white core:** the player stays readable inside the brightest moment. A cheap and
  strong look for a breakthrough or a sword finisher's impact.
- **Change the world for an instant:** a tinted sky and ground make a moment feel bigger than particles alone, at very
  little cost. Reduced Effects should turn it off.
- **Ribbons, then sparks:** sweeping curves first, a scatter of small sparks after, so the effect has a clear end.
