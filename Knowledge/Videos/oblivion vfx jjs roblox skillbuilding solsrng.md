## Key takeaways

What the frames show, read as lessons for our auras. These are my interpretation, not the creator's advice.

- **The silhouette is one idea:** a single burst above the head and one ring on the ground, with the small character
  between them. The scale gap makes the effect feel huge.
- **One colour family:** violet from near black to white. Brightness alone separates the layers.
- **A dark setting does much of the work:** the night scene lets a saturated colour glow without washing out. Our
  sky is bright, which is why the owner's aura needed careful tuning (see [[Egg Aura Studio Lessons]]).
- **A thin horizontal flare** through the core reads like a camera lens and adds a cinematic feel for very little.
- **Layers with separate jobs:** core, flare, shards, wisps and ground ripple each look distinct, in line with the
  owner's rule of fewer, well-tuned emitters.
- **Shapes change while the composition holds:** across about 13 seconds the outline keeps shifting and the
  centre stays put, so it stays alive without moving around.

**Use it as inspiration only.** The design rule is original auras that compete with Sol's RNG, never copies
([[Qi_Aura_Simulator_Design]], section 0). Aura work is also paused unless the owner picks it up again.
