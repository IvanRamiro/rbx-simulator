# Aura Sound Library

Sound effects found for the auras, first for the Realm 5 workbench storm (2026-11-04). Every pick is
from **ProSoundEffects**, Roblox's licensed library, free to use in any experience. Clips ripped from
other games (Half-Life, Undertale, Metroid, God of War, SCP and the like) turn up in every search
and are skipped: they aren't ours to use.

Durations are from the store listing or the loaded `TimeLength`. None have been listened to: the
owner judges them by ear. To swap one, change the Sound's `SoundId`.

Related: [[Smoke and Cloud Texture Library]], [[Work in Progress]].

the build, so it stays in sync with the visuals.
