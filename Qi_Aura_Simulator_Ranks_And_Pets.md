## 1. Game Overview

**Qi Aura Simulator** is an original Roblox cultivation simulator where
players train their inner Qi, advance through cultivation realms,
collect mystical creatures, hatch eggs, and explore floating islands
through free flight.

The two major progression systems are:

-   **Cultivation:** Determines the player's rank, power, progression
    milestones, and aura intensity.
-   **Pets:** Provide stat bonuses and allow players to customize their
    aura's visual appearance.

The goal is to make every rank-up and pet discovery feel rewarding.

## 2. Cultivation Progression System

The proposed system contains **12 major cultivation realms**, each with
**five sub-levels**, providing **60 progression levels**.

**Purpose:** Introduce players to Qi training and progression.

1.  Qi Initiate
2.  Qi Gatherer

### Cultivation design rules

-   Use five sub-levels per major realm.
-   Make every fifth level a noticeable milestone.
-   Give each major realm a distinct aura progression.
-   Consider unlocking new islands, eggs, and training areas at major
    milestones.
-   Keep rank requirements and multipliers in shared configuration.
-   Validate every rank-up on the server.
-   Implement only the first two or three realms for the initial
    playable prototype.

The names and effects are proposals and may be changed during
development.

## 4. Pet Rarity and Egg Chances

The following probabilities are a proposed starting point for balancing
one standard egg.

| Rarity | Example | Suggested chance |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Aqua Wisp | 45% |
| Uncommon | Ember Wyrm | 27% |
| Rare | Lunar Hopper | 15% |
| Epic | Volt Fox | 8% |
| Legendary | Golden Kirin | 4% |
| Mythical | Astral Whale | 0.9% |
| Secret | Origin Serpent | 0.1% |
| **Total** | | **100%** |

These are illustrative values, not finalized game balance. Each egg may
have its own pet pool, probabilities, and unlock requirements.

Rarity should not be the only measure of a pet's appeal. Common pets can
still have distinctive designs and attractive aura effects.

## 5. Egg Types and Locations

### Beginner Qi Egg

**Location:** Starting island

Contains beginner-friendly spirit animals and elemental creatures.
Introduces players to hatching and pet equipment.

### Jade Spirit Egg

**Location:** Forest island

Contains forest guardians, jade creatures, and nature-themed mythical
beasts.

### Inferno Egg

**Location:** Volcanic island

Contains fire dragons, magma creatures, and flame spirits.

### Celestial Egg

**Location:** Heavenly island

Contains celestial birds, cosmic beasts, and powerful spirit companions.

### Forbidden Origin Egg

**Location:** Hidden endgame island

Contains secret, ancient, and primordial creatures.

For the MVP, implement only the **Beginner Qi Egg**. Additional eggs can
be added when exploration and progression are functional.

## 6. Pet Equipment and Aura Rules

The initial system uses **three pet equipment slots**.

-   All equipped pets contribute their configured stat bonuses.
-   One equipped pet is selected as the **aura pet**.
-   The selected aura pet determines the player's aura appearance and
    theme.
-   Changing the aura pet does not remove the bonuses of the other
    equipped pets.
-   Pet inventory, equipment changes, and hatching results are
    controlled or validated by the server.

### Example build

A player could equip:

-   **Azure Sky Dragon** for a Qi bonus.
-   **Jade Spirit Panda** for a training bonus.
-   **Lunar Hopper** for a flight bonus.

The player could select one of those three pets as the visual aura pet.

Exact bonuses and stacking rules remain to be determined.

## 8. Recommended Development Order

### Stage 1 --- Basic Cultivation

-   Implement Qi accumulation.
-   Add active training.
-   Add a passive training station.
-   Create the first five sub-levels.
-   Implement server-validated rank-ups.

### Stage 2 --- Basic Pets

-   Create three original pet models.
-   Add a pet inventory.
-   Implement three equipment slots.
-   Add server-controlled stat bonuses.
-   Add aura-pet selection.

### Stage 3 --- Egg Hatching

-   Create the Beginner Qi Egg.
-   Define its pet pool and probabilities.
-   Implement server-side hatching.
-   Display the result to the player.
-   Test invalid requests and repeated hatching attempts.

### Stage 4 --- Aura Progression

-   Make aura intensity increase with cultivation.
-   Give each starter pet a distinct visual theme.
-   Ensure effects remain readable during multiplayer gameplay.

### Stage 5 --- Flight and Exploration

-   Implement free flight and a flight-speed upgrade.
-   Expand the initial floating island.
-   Add new egg locations and discoveries after the core loop works.

### Stage 6 --- Expansion

-   Add the remaining cultivation realms gradually.
-   Introduce additional pet families.
-   Add new islands and egg types.
-   Balance progression based on playtesting.

## 9. Design Principles

1.  Keep the core training loop satisfying before adding many systems.
2.  Use original models, names, and effects.
3.  Store rank and pet definitions in shared configuration.
4.  Validate progression, inventory, and rewards on the server.
5.  Keep pet appearance separate from pet stat bonuses.
6.  Test multiplayer behavior and saving before release.
7.  Avoid implementing every planned pet and rank at once.
8.  Treat all values in this document as adjustable until tested.

## 10. Project Vision

Qi Aura Simulator aims to become an original Roblox cultivation
experience where training increases power, cultivation unlocks new
possibilities, pets provide strategic bonuses, and unique auras allow
players to express their identity.

The long-term vision is a world of floating islands, ancient spirit
beasts, celestial discoveries, and increasingly powerful cultivators.

## 11. Implemented So Far

What is in the game today. All values live in `src/shared/GameConfig.lua`.

-   **Ranks:** Realms 1--3 (Qi Awakening, Qi Foundation, Spirit Core),
    15 levels from Qi Initiate (0 Qi) to Golden Spirit Core (120,000 Qi).
    Each rank sets an aura tier from 1 to 5. Reaching a fifth level or a
    new realm plays a bigger breakthrough effect.
-   **Pacing** (minutes of cultivating in the Qi Spring with typical
    pets): first rank-up after about 20 seconds, Qi Awakening done at
    about 4 minutes, Qi Foundation at about 24, Spirit Core at about 82.
    Run `python3 tools/balance_report.py` to see the timings for the
    current numbers after any change.
-   **Pets:** Fifteen pets, each with its own aura element and a small
    companion model. The Beginner Qi Egg's seven follow the concept art
    sheet (faceted crystal look, big glossy eyes, glowing accents):

| Ember Wyrm | Uncommon | +20% | Beginner Qi, Inferno | Orange flame tongues, embers, flames in the hands |
| Lunar Hopper | Rare | +35% | Beginner Qi | Lavender spirals, star motes, crescent halo |
| Volt Fox | Epic | +50% | Beginner Qi | Fast yellow shards, crackling arcs of electricity |

-   **Eggs:** Free to hatch, each with its own 45-second cooldown. Chances
    follow the guide's rarities; an egg without a pet of some rarity
    gives its share to the next rarer pet it has.

| Egg | Where | Needs | Chances |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner Qi Egg | Starting island | Any rank | Aqua Wisp 45%, Ember Wyrm 27%, Lunar Hopper 15%, Volt Fox 8%, Golden Kirin 4%, Astral Whale 0.9%, Origin Serpent 0.1% |
| Jade Spirit Egg | Jade Grove | Qi Gatherer | Moss Golem 45%, Mycelium Sprite 27%, Jade Spirit Panda 15%, Jade Antler Deer 8%, Golden Kirin 5% |
| Inferno Egg | Ember Peak | Qi Awakener | Magma Cub 45%, Ember Wyrm 27%, Sunscale Dragon 23%, Dawn Phoenix 5% |

-   **Pet companions:** Every equipped pet appears as a small original
    low-poly model (faceted octagonal bodies, wedge ears, wings and tails,
    built from parts in code, no uploaded meshes) that floats behind its
    owner and circles them while they cultivate. Everyone sees everyone's
    pets.
-   **Qi Spring (training station):** A glowing pool on the starting island,
    across from the egg. Cultivating while standing in it adds +50% Qi on
    top of pet bonuses; the HUD shows when the bonus is active.
-   **Flight:** Unlocks at Qi Gatherer. Q (gamepad Y, or the Fly button)
    takes off and lands; players fly where the camera looks with their
    normal movement controls, and Space / Ctrl climb and descend. Ranking
    up makes flight faster: 28 studs/s at Qi Gatherer, then 34, 40, 50, 58
    and 70 at Golden Spirit Core (walking is 16). Taking off ends
    cultivating, and players must land to cultivate.
-   **Sky islands:** Three islands to fly to around the starting island:
    Jade Grove (forest, east), Ember Peak (volcanic with lava pools,
    south-west) and Moonlit Isle (pale and celestial, the highest).
-   **Spirit Relics:** Nine glowing relics to find, one hidden under the
    starting island and the rest on the sky islands (one floats high above
    Ember Peak). Attuning to each gives a one-time Qi reward, 13,200 Qi in
    total: 300 under the starting island, 800 each on Jade Grove (from Qi
    Gatherer), 1,500 each on Ember Peak (from Qi Awakener) and 3,000 each
    on Moonlit Isle (from Foundation Initiate). The HUD counts found
    relics, found ones fade for that player, and finds are saved.
-   **Nine-Tail Moonfox** no longer hatches (Astral Whale took its place in
    the Beginner Qi Egg); players who own one keep it.
-   **New players** start with an Aqua Wisp.
-   **Older saves** are upgraded automatically. Each old rank becomes the
    highest new rank that needs no more Qi than it did (Qi is kept, so
    players can rank straight back up). Old pets become the new pet of the
    same rarity: Ember Wisp → Aqua Wisp, Moon Koi → Ember Wyrm, Jade
    Tortoise → Volt Fox. Equipped pets and the aura pet stay selected.
